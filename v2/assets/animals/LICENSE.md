Changes for Artificial Evolution 2: reconstructed PBR materials from legacy Blender textures; normalized heading, origin and scale; retained deer animations; authored in-place idle/run poses on the wolf rig; baked the rigs into GPU position/normal textures; added animation lookup UVs; and removed runtime skinning and unused bone nodes. Additional skeletal actions, shared evaluated bone poses, GPU bone matrices, and clip transitions were prepared for event-driven animation. These derived animal animation assets are also CC0. The animation rigs are editable in `v2/art_source/animation/`. The original rigged models remain editable in `v2/art_source/animals/`. These derived model and animation assets are also dedicated under CC0 1.0.

Source URLs, creators, original download SHA-256 checksums and hashes of the derived assets are listed in [ASSET_SOURCES.json](ASSET_SOURCES.json). Project scripts and shaders are MIT licensed under `v2/LICENSE`. Blender is an external authoring tool and is not bundled. The simulated animals are illustrative representations of Python agents, rather than biological models of these species.
