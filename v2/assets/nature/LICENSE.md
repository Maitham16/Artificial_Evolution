The photographic textures and scanned tree, plant and rock models in this directory originate from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com). Poly Haven dedicates its assets to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); see its [asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license).

The optimized meshes and baked tree images are derived from those CC0 assets and are distributed under the same CC0 dedication. Attribution is appreciated although CC0 does not require it. Author names, asset pages, download URLs and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in [ASSET_SOURCES.json](ASSET_SOURCES.json).
