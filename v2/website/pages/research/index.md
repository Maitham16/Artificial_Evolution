# Investigate behavior with evidence

Artificial Evolution asks whether inherited neural controllers develop useful behavior under defined conditions. The supported conclusion is **evolved adaptive behavior in specified experiments**, rather than unrestricted or general intelligence.

## Distinguish three kinds of evidence

| Evidence | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Live demonstration | What happened in one configured population? |
| Engineering verification | Does the implementation preserve its declared behavior and numerical record? |
| Independent-seed study | Does a treatment improve an endpoint under matched conditions, with uncertainty and failures reported? |

Screenshots, animations and replay are useful observations, but repeated frames or many animals within one training seed are not independent evolutionary replications.

Separate training and test environments, aggregate within independent training seeds, and report paired effects, confidence intervals, statistical decisions and failure cases according to each protocol.
